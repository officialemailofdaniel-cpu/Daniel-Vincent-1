What to know before you start

This tool rebuilds pages as images, which can reduce selectable text, accessibility, vector sharpness or image quality. Keep the original if you need searchable text or high-fidelity printing.

Frequently asked questions

Will every PDF become smaller?

Not always. Results depend on the original document, page content, scale and image-quality settings.

Does compression preserve selectable text?

This image-based compression may not preserve selectable text.

Keep your original document until you have checked the downloaded result. File size, browser memory and document structure can affect processing. Do not use a PDF crop or watermark as a substitute for secure redaction of confidential information.
