What to know before you start

Page numbers use the original document order. Check the selected range before downloading, especially for long documents.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Enter comma-separated page numbers and ranges, for example 1-2,5,8-10.

Does extraction modify the original?

No. The selected pages are copied into a new output PDF.

Keep your original document until you have checked the downloaded result. File size, browser memory and document structure can affect processing. Do not use a PDF crop or watermark as a substitute for secure redaction of confidential information.
