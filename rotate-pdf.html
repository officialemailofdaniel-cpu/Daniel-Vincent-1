How to use Rotate PDF

Correct sideways or upside-down PDF pages by choosing a rotation amount and downloading the updated document.

What to know before you start

The current tool applies the selected rotation to every page. Check the document before processing if only one page needs correction.

Frequently asked questions

Will rotation change my original?

No. A new rotated PDF is downloaded; your original is not overwritten.

Which rotations are available?

The tool offers 90°, 180° and 270° rotation options.

Keep your original document until you have checked the downloaded result. File size, browser memory and document structure can affect processing. Do not use a PDF crop or watermark as a substitute for secure redaction of confidential information.
