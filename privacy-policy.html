Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyPDFFree (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit mypdffree.com and use its online PDF tools.

PDF files and tool use

MyPDFFree provides browser-based PDF tools. The tools in this version are designed to process files in your browser rather than upload them to a MyPDFFree file-processing server. Your browser may load supporting libraries and resources from third-party content delivery networks. Do not use the site for highly sensitive documents if you are unsure whether a tool is suitable for your needs.

Information you provide

You can use the tools without creating an account. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry, and use it to respond.

Cookies and advertising

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functions and, if advertising or analytics services are enabled, to support those services. Third-party providers, including Google if Google advertising is displayed, may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own policies and settings. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and available advertising privacy controls.

Third-party services

The site may load third-party libraries or services to provide its tools. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their resources. Please review the relevant provider policies for details.

Data security and children's privacy

Use an up-to-date browser and device, and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily. MyPDFFree is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when changes were made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact MyPDFFree through the Contact page.
