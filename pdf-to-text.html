How to use PDF to Text

Extract selectable text from a PDF for copying, searching or reuse. The tool places extracted text in a text area.

What to know before you start

Extraction works best when a PDF contains a real text layer. Scanned pages may not contain selectable text; use OCR for image-based documents. Tables and columns may not retain their original layout.

Frequently asked questions

Why is no text extracted?

The PDF may be scanned or image-only. Try the OCR tool for scanned pages.

Will formatting be preserved?

Complex layouts and tables may not retain their original formatting.

Keep your original document until you have checked the downloaded result. File size, browser memory and document structure can affect processing. Do not use a PDF crop or watermark as a substitute for secure redaction of confidential information.
